Computation plays a growing role in both my research and my teaching. These are the tools I use most.

## In research

- **Python:** I developed a Python implementation that computes the growth rates of quantum invariants of glued 3-manifolds for $r$ up to 20,000 in under a minute, giving numerical evidence for the volume conjecture where no theorem exists yet. I plan to release it as a documented open-source package for Reshetikhin–Turaev and Turaev–Viro invariants. See [Quantum Low-Dimensional Topology](/research1/project1/).
- **LaTeX:** for papers, slides (Beamer), and figures.
- **GitHub:** for version control and sharing code.
- **Lean:** a proof assistant I am exploring for formal, precise proof writing.

## In teaching

- **LaTeX in the classroom:** integrated into Introduction to Proofs to develop students' mathematical communication.
- **Microsoft Whiteboard, D2L, Gradescope, and WeBWorK:** for collaborative online group work, course organization, feedback, and homework.

## Workshops and mentoring

- **Resource person, LaTeX workshop**, Sri Lanka (2024).
- **RTG MSU Summer Topology Program (2023):** supervised two undergraduate students' mathematical writing and LaTeX skills as they prepared research write-ups and presentations.
